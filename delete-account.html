Versla – Delete your account and data

Versla is an Android shopping-list app published on Google Play by Anthony Mirely. This page explains how to ask for your Versla account and the data linked to it to be deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to anthonymirely@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Versla account.
  2. Send it from the email address you used to sign up for Versla, so the request can be matched to your account.
  3. You will get a reply confirming the deletion once it is done, within 30 days of your request.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your name, email address and sign-in credentials.
  • The shopping lists you created and the items in them.
  • The photos you attached to items.

What is kept

  • Items you added to a list owned by someone else stay in that list, without your account details.
  • Usage statistics collected through Firebase Analytics that are not linked to your account may be kept in aggregated form.

Deleting only some of your data

You can delete individual lists, items and photos yourself inside the app at any time. To have specific data removed without deleting your account, email the address above and describe what you want removed.
